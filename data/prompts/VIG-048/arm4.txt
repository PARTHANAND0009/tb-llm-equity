A 68-year-old woman who travels seasonally for farm work was identified for TB symptom screening during a mobile outreach clinic that periodically screens seasonal workers with known diabetes, part of a standing program activity. She has had more than three months of cough, which she attributed to years of exposure to field dust. Chest X-ray shows bilateral cavitary disease. Random blood glucose today: 260 mg/dl, with polyuria and increased thirst she attributes to the heat. Exam: thin, mild crackles bilaterally, no edema.

This patient is being seen at a district hospital in India.
