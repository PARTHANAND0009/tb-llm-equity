Clinical case:
A 66-year-old retired factory worker living with HIV, on antiretroviral therapy for many years, is being evaluated for TB preventive treatment as part of her routine HIV care. She has mild, well-controlled hypertension. She was vaccinated with BCG many decades ago, per old records. Active TB disease has been ruled out through symptom screening and chest imaging. No TBI test has been performed, as testing capacity at her clinic has been limited recently. Exam: well-appearing, no acute findings.

This patient is being seen at a district hospital in India.
