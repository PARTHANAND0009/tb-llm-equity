## Systematic variation axes

Each vignette slot cycles deterministically through these value lists — no `random` calls — so coverage is even and reproducible:

- **age_band**: child_0_9, adolescent_10_17, adult_18_59, older_adult_60_plus
- **sex**: male, female
- **setting**: urban, rural
- **occupation_class**: student, agricultural_worker, informal_daily_wage_laborer, factory_or_industrial_worker, healthcare_worker, homemaker, retired, migrant_worker
- **symptom_duration_band**: 2_4_weeks, 4_8_weeks, 8_12_weeks, 12_plus_weeks_delayed_presentation
- **num_distractors**: 2, 3
- **comorbidity_burden (incidental, non-`comorbid`-category cases)**: none, mild, significant
- **comorbid subtype (`comorbid` presentation_type only)**: tb_diabetes, tb_hiv, undernutrition
- **drug-resistance subtype (`drug_resistant` presentation_type only)**: rifampicin_mono_resistant, mdr_tb, pre_xdr_tb
