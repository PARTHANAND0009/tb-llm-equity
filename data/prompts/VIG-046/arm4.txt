She reports about six weeks of cough and worsening fatigue, which she and her family attributed to her usual HIV-related tiredness. Her most recent CD4 count, checked a month ago, was 85 cells/uL. No known TB contact in the household. No prior TB treatment, no known drug-resistant contact. Chest X-ray shows bilateral infiltrates with early cavitation. This patient is being seen at a district hospital in India.
