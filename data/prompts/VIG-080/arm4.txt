Clinical case:
A 68-year-old woman who travels between seasonal work sites presents after more than three months of cough and weight loss, delayed by her travel schedule and difficulty accessing consistent care while moving between locations. She has mild, longstanding vision impairment managed with glasses. No prior TB treatment, no known contact with drug-resistant TB, no HIV risk factors. Sputum obtained on request was of poor quality. Exam: thin, bilateral crackles.

This patient is being seen at a teaching hospital in the United States.
