A 2-year-old girl is brought in by her father, a paramedic, after about three weeks of cough and low-grade fevers. She has a history of mild, recurrent ear infections, most recently resolved a month ago. She has not been eating as well as usual. No known TB contact in the household. No prior TB treatment, no known drug-resistant contact. A gastric aspirate, obtained after she could not produce sputum, was positive for M. tuberculosis on molecular testing. This patient is being seen at a teaching hospital in the United States.
