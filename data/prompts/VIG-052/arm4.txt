In 2023, among 25.52 lakh TB patients diagnosed, 24.38 lakh (95.5%) patients were put on treatment (4).

current standard of care for drug susceptible TB i.e. 6-month regimen (two months initiation phase consists
of isoniazid [H], rifampicin [R], pyrazinamide [Z], ethambutol [E] followed by four months HRE), are 85% and
87%, respectively (5).
