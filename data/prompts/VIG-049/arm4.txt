A 6-year-old boy living with perinatally acquired HIV, on antiretroviral therapy followed at a pediatric clinic, was identified for TB symptom screening during a routine outreach visit that systematically checks children living with HIV for TB, part of a standing program activity. His grandmother, his primary caregiver, reports about three weeks of intermittent cough and reduced playfulness, which she had not thought serious enough to bring him in for on her own. No known TB contact in the household. No prior TB treatment, no known drug-resistant contact. Chest X-ray shows a left hilar lymphadenopathy pattern.
