Clinical case:
A 15-year-old girl who works long hours in the fields presents with about seven weeks of intermittent right lower abdominal pain, low-grade fevers, and a noticeable reduction in appetite. Her family had assumed it was ongoing digestive trouble from 'eating on the run' during long field days. She has longstanding significant undernutrition with short stature for her age, previously noted at a health visit but not further investigated. No known TB contact in the household. No prior TB treatment, no known drug-resistant contact. Abdominal exam reveals a vague, mildly tender right lower quadrant mass; imaging shows ileocecal wall thickening with regional lymphadenopathy. Ultrasound-guided aspiration of an enlarged regional lymph node was sent for molecular testing, which was positive for M. tuberculosis. Exam: mildly tender right lower quadrant fullness, no rebound or guarding, significantly underweight and short for age.

This patient is being seen at a district hospital in India.
